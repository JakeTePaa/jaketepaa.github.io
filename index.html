Process

How a project runs

Three stages, the same for a single campaign or a full launch.

  1. Research

    I learn your brand, your customers and the Australian market: what competitors post, what local audiences respond to, and where the gaps are.

  2. Build

    I set up the AI workflows, the content calendar and the channel plan, so good content goes out on schedule every week.

  3. Grow

    We launch, measure and put more behind what works. You get a plain monthly report showing where the money went and what it returned.
